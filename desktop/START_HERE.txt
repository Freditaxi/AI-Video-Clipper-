YOUR INDEPENDENT AI CLIPPER
No vidIQ account, subscription, or credits required.

1. Install Python 3.12 (64-bit):
   https://www.python.org/downloads/
   Check "Add Python to PATH" when installing.

2. Right-click this ZIP and choose "Extract All".

3. Inside the extracted folder, double-click START_WINDOWS.bat.
   Leave its terminal window open.

4. Your browser opens the local app at:
   http://127.0.0.1:8765

5. Choose a video file. For your first test, select a short
   section and use "Fast - Tiny English". Click Create my clips.

The first setup downloads free software and model files.
Later runs reuse those models. Your computer does the work.
You do not need to sign in or enter a payment method.

The app runs on your laptop, not directly on an iPhone.
MP4 clips can be transferred to your phone after creation.
This release exports clips; you upload them yourself.

Read README.md for detailed help and known limitations.
